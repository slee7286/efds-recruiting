# Shared versus local data

Public companies, jobs, source documents, claims, recruiting intelligence,
skills, and resources are `SHARED_PUBLIC`. Candidate profile/evidence, private
applications, artifacts, answers, AI tasks/results, review history, browser
runs, prep history, notes, and outcomes are `LOCAL_PRIVATE`.

Mixed records retain shared UUIDs locally but keep private annotations in local
tables. AI conversation text is `AI_CONTEXT`, not authoritative evidence.
