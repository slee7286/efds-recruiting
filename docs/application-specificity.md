# Application specificity rubric

Future written answers may use this rubric, but V3 does not score semantics:

* 0 — generic recruiting language
* 1 — industry-specific
* 2 — company-specific
* 3 — team, programme, or role-specific
* 4 — cites identifiable company evidence
* 5 — connects identifiable company evidence to candidate evidence

`application_answers.specificity_score` is nullable and intended for a later
review or interactive-AI workflow. Candidate evidence remains the source of
truth for personal achievements.
