# Recruiting intelligence

Role families are relational and hierarchical, so quantitative research,
investment banking, software engineering, consulting, and other families share
the same model without forcing one interview process onto another. Interview
stages are also relational and include assessments, case interviews,
assessment centres, technical and behavioural interviews, offers, and
rejections.

Firm intelligence items (themes, values, teams, programmes, and talking points)
are manual/deterministic foundations with source associations. Candidate-firm
matches are private, explicitly scored, and approval-aware; no generated
personal claims are stored automatically.
